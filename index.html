StarNet Etsy Research

A private research and planning application used to study permitted Etsy marketplace information and help develop original products for one future seller business.

What the application does

  • Reviews publicly available shop and listing information through Etsy's approved API.
  • Identifies broad market patterns such as product categories, price ranges, personalization options, and listing structures.
  • Supports internal planning for original products, listings, and marketing experiments.

Who uses it

The application is for private use by its owner and authorized internal collaborators. It is not offered as a public service or sold to other Etsy sellers.

Data and intellectual-property boundaries

The application does not scrape Etsy, access private seller information without authorization, or reproduce another seller's artwork, photography, files, branding, or listing copy. Research is used to inform clean-room development of original products and materials.

API use

Access is limited to endpoints, data, and permissions authorized by Etsy and the connected account. Credentials and authorization tokens are kept private and are never displayed on this site.
